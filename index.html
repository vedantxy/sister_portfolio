About Me

A passionate and detail-oriented medical laboratory professional in training, driven by curiosity and a commitment to healthcare excellence.

I am a dedicated Medical Laboratory Assistant and Technician student at Trillium College, bringing together a strong foundation in microbiology and a genuine passion for laboratory science.
